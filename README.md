🔫 Modern warfare meets Minecraft – for free!

Block Ops: Warfare is a military-themed Minecraft modpack inspired by classic FPS games like Call of Duty – completely reimagined in the world of blocks. Built by Dark ˣ Pixel Studios™, this pack delivers fast-paced action, tactical decision-making, and immersive multiplayer gameplay – 100% free.

⚠️Please make sure to allocate at least 8–10 GB of RAM to your Minecraft, or to your server if you are hosting one.

🧱 Core Features:

📝 No server needed, just invite your friends to a single-player world!
✅ Standard kits with unlockable upgrades
💰 In-game currency & fully functional weapon shop
🔥 Killstreaks with powerful rewards
🗺️ Custom maps built for intense PvP
🔫 Realistic modern weapons with detailed mechanics
🎯 Training areas and shooting ranges
👥 Team selection system
⚔️ Game modes: Team Deathmatch, Deathmatch, Gun Game
🛡️ Designed for multiplayer, but also works in singleplayer
🔄 Regular updates based on community feedback

Whether you're dominating the battlefield or training for the next round, Block Ops: Warfare offers an FPS-style experience with Minecraft’s creativity and flexibility.
